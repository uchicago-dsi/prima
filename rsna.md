## Submission / Compliance Points

- The RSNA public page states that authors retain copyright and grant RSNA a limited, nonexclusive, royalty-free license to present and archive accepted content.
- Financial disclosure is required for people in control of educational content.
- Content must be fair, balanced, and free of commercial bias.

## Scientific Abstract Formatting: Confirmed 2026

- Source file downloaded locally: `2026_RSNA_Abstract_Submission_Guidelines.pdf`
- Required scientific abstract headings: Purpose; Materials and Methods; Results; Conclusion; Clinical Relevance Statement.
- Scientific abstract limit: 2400 characters, not including spaces.
- Important correction from earlier notes: the 2026 guideline PDF does not state a separate 200-character limit for the Clinical Relevance Statement. It lists Clinical Relevance Statement as one of the required scientific abstract headings inside the 2400-character no-spaces limit.
- A supporting figure is recommended for review purposes.
- Quality Improvement Reports should be submitted under the Science category of Noninterpretive Skills / Beyond Imaging.
- Scientific presentations may be accepted as oral paper presentations, digital poster presentations, or invited hardcopy backboard presentations at RSNA's discretion.

## Education Abstract Formatting: Confirmed 2026

- Education abstract limit: 1500 characters.
- Required education headings: Teaching Points; Table of Contents/Outline; PDF Upload.
- A PDF upload containing five figures must accompany the education abstract submission.
