Target view only: decide whether the visual target "visible breast implant" is present in this mammogram.

Answer YES when any part of a breast implant prosthesis is visibly present. This includes a large smooth rounded or curved implant contour, the implant shell, or a partially displaced posterior implant on an implant-displaced/Eklund view. The implant does not need to occupy most of the image.

Answer NO for normal breast or pectoral contours, routine compression paddles or detector edges, tissue expanders without a visible breast prosthesis, ports or pacemakers, localization wires, biopsy or surgical clips, skin markers, text labels, and detector artifacts. Small clips and other ordinary post-procedure findings are not breast implants.

Use high confidence only when an implant contour or shell is unmistakable. Use medium or low confidence when a smooth curved edge could instead be anatomy or imaging hardware, and set REVIEW: YES for genuinely ambiguous cases.
