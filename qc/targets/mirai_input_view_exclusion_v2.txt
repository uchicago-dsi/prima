Answer NO, meaning USE FOR MIRAI, for an ordinary native-digital whole-breast CC or MLO view without a disqualifying problem. Pathology is not a QC failure: do not reject an otherwise usable view merely because it contains cancer, a mass, asymmetry, architectural distortion, calcifications, dense tissue, scars, or other post-surgical breast changes. Do not impose strict ACR positioning criteria.

Film is a separate failure rule. A definite physical film frame plus film labels, uneven illumination/fog, scratches/dust, or scanned-film texture supports YES. Old age, low contrast, a black border, or one text label alone does not prove film.
